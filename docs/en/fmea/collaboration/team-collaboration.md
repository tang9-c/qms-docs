# Team Collaboration

Team collaboration is an important part of FMEA analysis. Through role and permission management and electronic signature features, it ensures team members can collaborate efficiently. This guide explains how to use the team collaboration features.

## Roles & Permissions

The system provides 6 standard roles: Promoter, Technical Lead, Decision Maker, etc. Each role has different permissions and responsibilities.

## Electronic Signature

Approvers can review online and add electronic signatures, locking the current version to ensure the seriousness and traceability of FMEA analysis.
