# Change Management

Change management is an important part of ensuring that all FMEA documents (family/projects) content remains synchronized with the product design, manufacturing process, and actual risks they describe. This guide explains how to perform change management.

## Initiate Change

Any project member can initiate a change.

![Initiate Change](/images/wps54.png)

### Fill in Change Application

![Fill in Change Application](/images/wps55.png)

![Change Application Details](/images/wps56.png)

Click "Submit" to initiate change approval.

## Change Approval

![Change Approval](/images/wps57.png)

![Change Approval Details](/images/wps58.png)

After the change application is approved, the project team can continue editing from Structure Analysis.

## Version Management

After the change is released, the version will be updated and historical versions will be recorded.

![Version Management](/images/wps59.png)
