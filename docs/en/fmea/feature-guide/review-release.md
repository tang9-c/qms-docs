# Review & Release

Review and release is the seventh step of FMEA analysis. After the intelligent team review passes, the goal is to aggregate FMEA analysis process related data and generate reports. This guide explains how to perform review and release.

## Functional Team Review

After risk analysis is completed, 【Functional Team Review】 role members need to conduct a comprehensive review of the entire FMEA project and submit approval opinions.

![Functional Team Review](/images/wps50.png)

![Functional Team Review Details](/images/wps51.png)

## Important Process Control

All 【Functional Team Review】 role members must complete approval. Only when all review members disagree will the project be finally released, forming a stable official version.

## Results & Documentation

After the review team approves: Automatically aggregate forms and generate reports, export standard format FMEA tables, risk matrix charts, or measure tracking tables with one click.
