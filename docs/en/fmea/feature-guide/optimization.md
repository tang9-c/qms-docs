# Optimization Measures

Optimization is the sixth step of FMEA analysis, with the goal of developing measures and tracking results. This guide explains how to develop and manage optimization measures.

## Develop Optimization Measures

- **Goal**: Develop measures and track results.
- **Operations**:
  - For High/Medium AP items: Click "Mark for Optimization"

    ![Mark for Optimization](/images/wps47.png)

  - In the optimization measure list, assign responsible person and completion date, initiate task

    ![Assign Responsible Person](/images/wps48.png)

  - Measure status: Pending -> In Progress -> Completed -> Verified.

## Important Process Control

After risk analysis is completed, the 【Project Manager】 role member must submit for review before the project can proceed to the 【Functional Team Review】 node.

![Submit for Review](/images/wps49.png)
